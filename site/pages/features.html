Everything the YouTube video summarizer does, and why. Most tools hand you the same words in a different box — everything here exists to get you from a link to something you can use, and to let you check it in one click.

Four summary depths from one run

Different moments call for different amounts of detail. A three-hour podcast you're triaging needs one line; a lecture you're revising needs the whole structure. You get both from the same run — switching views costs nothing and never reprocesses the video.

  • TL;DR — two or three sentences carrying the actual argument
  • Key takeaways — five to eight specific, checkable claims
  • Chapters — the shape of the video, each linked to its timestamp
  • Full notes — headed sections you can paste into a notes app

Timestamped chapters that link back to the video

Every chapter is a real link into the video at the second it starts. Skim the outline, spot the part you actually needed, and jump straight there instead of scrubbing a progress bar for five minutes.

This is also what makes the summary checkable. If a claim looks wrong or too convenient, the timestamp next to it takes you to the moment it was said. Verification is a click, not a rewatch.

Quotes stay quotes

Numbers, names, dates and direct quotes carry across as they were said, not smoothed into an approximation. Where a speaker hedges, the hedge survives. Where the video contradicts itself, you get told rather than handed a tidy average of two incompatible claims.

A summary is a starting point, not a substitute for the source. The design goal here is to make checking it fast enough that you actually do.

Summarize YouTube videos in 38 languages

The source language never has to be your language. Summarize a Japanese lecture into English, an English keynote into Spanish, or a German conference talk into whatever you take notes in.

Technical vocabulary is the hard part of cross-language summarization, so terms of art are kept in their original form alongside the translation rather than being guessed at.

More YouTube summarizer features

Export anywhere

Copy as clean Markdown or download a .md file named after the video. Drops straight into Notion, Obsidian, or a pull request.

Any URL shape

youtube.com/watch, youtu.be, Shorts, embeds, live replays, youtube-nocookie, or a bare video id — all recognised.

Built for keyboards

/ focuses the input, Ctrl+Enter runs it, arrow keys move between summary views. No reaching for the mouse.

Recent history

Your last summaries stay a click away. Without an account they live in this browser only — clearing them clears them for good.

Light and dark

Follows your system by default, or pick one and it sticks. Applied before the page paints, so there's no flash of the wrong theme.

Shareable links

Every summary has a URL. Send /?v=VIDEO_ID to a colleague and they land on the same result.
