Billing

Billing and plan questions

Is the free plan a trial?

No. It's a real plan with no end date and no card required. Five summaries a day is enough for most people who watch a couple of long videos a week.

Can I cancel whenever I want?

Yes, from your account settings, without emailing anyone. Monthly plans run to the end of the period you've paid for. Yearly plans are refunded pro rata for the months you haven't used.

What happens to my summaries if I downgrade?

They stay readable and exportable. You keep access to everything you've already generated — the plan only governs what you can create from that point on.

Do you offer student or education pricing?

Yes. Get in touch from your institutional email address and we'll sort it out. Course-wide and departmental licences are handled the same way.

What if I need more than five Team seats?

Additional seats are added to the Team plan at a per-seat rate. For larger rollouts, SSO, or a procurement process, talk to us.
