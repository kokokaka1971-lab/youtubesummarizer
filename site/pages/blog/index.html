What we write about

Three things, mostly. The first is how to actually learn from video — what the research on retrieval practice says, why rewatching a lecture feels productive and mostly isn't, and where a summary helps rather than getting in the way.

The second is what separates a summary from a transcript. A lot of tools labelled "YouTube summarizer" hand back the caption file with paragraph breaks, and the difference between that and something you'd revise from is most of the work.

The third is verification — why every claim should carry the timestamp it came from, and what goes wrong in summarization tools that drop the timings. It's the thing we care most about, and the reason this product is built the way it is.

Nothing here is a press release. If you want the product, the summarizer is on the homepage and takes about twenty seconds.
