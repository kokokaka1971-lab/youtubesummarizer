# Content calendar - YouTubeSummarizer.com

Built from `ahrefs/youtubesummarizer_1000_keyword_topical_map.xlsx` (1,000 keywords,
20 clusters x 50). Ten batches of ten pages.

## The rule that shapes this plan

The map's own SEO Strategy sheet says: *"Do not create one page for every keyword.
Group keywords by the same search intent and use one strong page for each distinct
intent to avoid cannibalization."*

So this is **100 pages for 1,000 keywords** - roughly ten keyword variants absorbed
per page. The `free` / `online` / `without login` modifiers are headings and FAQ
entries on a pillar, not separate URLs.

## What the site already covers

Five clusters already have a hub. Do not build a competing page for these:

| Cluster | Existing page | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Core YouTube Summarizer | `/` | Leave alone - it is the money page |
| Students & Study | `/use-cases/students` | Add supporting pages (batch 4) |
| Research & Professionals | `/use-cases/researchers` | Add supporting pages (batch 6) |
| Creators & Marketers | `/use-cases/creators` | Add supporting pages (batch 7) |
| How-To & Troubleshooting | `/how-it-works` | Add supporting pages (batches 5, 10) |

The other **15 clusters have no hub at all**. That is what batches 1-2 fix.

## Sequencing

Batch order follows the map's suggested first wave (Core -> Free/no-signup ->
Transcript -> Students -> Long videos -> Timestamps -> Key points -> Notes ->
Podcasts -> Lectures -> Comparisons), with one change: **every pillar ships before
any supporting page**. A supporting page with no hub to link up to is an orphan.

## Before you write a word of batch 1

The map's Validation note says the competition labels are *strategic long-tail
estimates*, not measured data:

> Before production, validate search volume, SERP strength, and keyword difficulty
> in Ahrefs/Semrush/Keyword Planner/Search Console.

Run that check on the ten batch-1 focus keywords first. If one turns out to be
zero-volume or dominated by entrenched domains, swap it rather than spending an
article on it.

## Honest risks in this plan

- **Cannibalization is the main danger.** `free youtube summarizer no signup`,
  `youtube summarizer without login` and `youtube video summarizer free` are one
  intent. They are one page here, with the variants as sections. Splitting them
  later will cost more than merging them now.
- **Batch 9 describes features that may not exist.** Vimeo, TikTok, Twitch and MP4
  pages should ship only if the product genuinely handles them. Pages promising
  absent features are the fastest route to the thin-content problem the map warns
  about.
- **Comparison articles (batch 3) name competitors.** They need real testing to be
  defensible, and they age. Budget for re-testing, not just writing.
- **100 pages at 1,200-1,400 words is ~130,000 words.** At 10 pages per batch that
  is a real programme, not a weekend.
